# The Long Travail 0.1.3

Release date: Unreleased

This update adds Liquefied Abundance, Immortal Bud, Coloring, three language options and compatibility presets for multiple configuration pools, and improves mod compatibility, configuration readability and recovery, and loot validation.

## Sun Chaser and Kagotsurube

- Added **Sun Chaser**, a Far Reach carried item: prevents ignition and fire/lava damage, restores 1 health per second in lava, and exempts the carrier from Abyss Malice periodic fluid damage while in lava. Malice reflects incoming projectiles and grants +30% projectile damage and +20% damage against burning targets. Witness suppresses normal healing and hurt cooldowns for 10 seconds and adds a direct strike equal to 2.5% of burning targets' maximum health, without further bonuses or critical hits.
- Obtain Sun Chaser from a master fletcher for 1 Icarus and 1 Far Reach Essence, or with a 5% chance from vanilla archaeology.
- Added **Kagotsurube**, an Underworld belt: each confirmed damage instance applies 5 seconds of Wither I or extends existing Wither by 5 seconds without changing its level. Malice grants +15 Insight and +0.1 Overlord; Witness converts damage attributed to the wearer into direct strikes. Crafted with a cursed enchanted book, Underworld Essence, a Netherite Sword and two Wither Roses.
- Malice and Witness abilities are separate; both retain base effects. Added Shiyi details, flavor descriptions and text in all five supported languages.
- Sun Chaser and Kagotsurube now work in native maid bauble slots. Base effects apply to the maid; Malice and Witness follow the Long Travail equipped by the online owner.

## Liquefied Abundance

Added **Liquefied Abundance**, an Abyss item stacking to 64. Tap to cycle normal, splash and lingering types, then hold for 1 second by default to create a random potion of the selected type. Releasing in between cancels. Selection is saved per player and shown in the item tooltip, with a thin charge bar below the prompt above the hotbar, matching the diary hint style.

- Includes registered vanilla and modded status effects with a configurable blacklist. Randomly selects existing potion levels and their associated durations; effects without potion samples default to level I for a configurable 15 seconds.
- Shapelessly craft 4 Liquefied Abundance with 1 honey bottle, 3 glass bottles, 1 milk bucket and 1 lapis lazuli; only the empty bucket is returned. Alternatively, brew dragon’s breath in the bottle slots with a lapis lazuli block above.
- Consumes one per conversion except in Creative mode; includes a hold progress indicator and Shiyi instructions.

## Active skill cooldowns

- Call Rain defaults to 120 seconds (previously 300); dream entry defaults to 30 seconds (previously 60). Durations remain 60 and 10 seconds respectively. Existing configuration values are preserved.

## Immortal Bud

Added **Immortal Bud**, a Flourishing item crafted shapelessly from one sapling, one bone meal, and one Everlasting Flower.

- Craft it shapelessly with a damageable item to consume one bud, fully repair the target, and grant **Evergreen**. The original name, enchantments, and other data are preserved. Marked items cannot receive the mark again.
- Evergreen protects against ordinary durability loss and breakage caused by an excessive single durability cost.
- The bud itself and protected items resist fire, lava, and explosions when dropped, and do not naturally despawn. Crafting, placement, and use still consume items normally; items lost to the void cannot be recovered.
- Full instructions are available in the Shiyi catalog. Protected items display an Evergreen line with the Flourishing theme below their names.

## Coloring and the Shiyi Catalog

Added **Coloring**, crafted with eight glowstone dust around a vanilla brush. Right-click while holding it to open its interface, place items in the left slot, select a style, and collect them from the right slot. Entire stacks can be processed at once.

- **Name styling**: Choose from seven theme styles plus Gilded and Ceramic, for nine effects in total. Only the appearance of item names changes; name text, lore, enchantments, and attributes are preserved.
- **Restore Style**: Removes The Long Travail's item-name rendering effects while preserving Apotheosis rarity colors.
- **Catalog**: Added Coloring and its instructions to the utility item section. Aligned its four items with the columns below and improved wrapping of closing punctuation in descriptions.

## Acquisition Changes

- Eki and Tokaido now come from fishing treasure alongside Perfect Fluid, each with a 10% share of treasure results.
- Lorentz Fantasy costs 16 Dragon's Breath and one Dragon Head; Phosphophyllite costs 64 Lapis Lazuli and one Green Dye. Existing master villagers receive the updated ingredients.
- Removed Free and Easy Wandering from stronghold chests. Moved Book of the Dead from stronghold libraries to Nether Fortress chests. Removed Azrael from Ancient City chests and Icarus from End City chests.
- Moved A Thousand Years Later from Nether Fortress to Woodland Mansion chests. Added Perfect Fluid to shipwreck supply chests at 5%, with Midnight Lafite at 5%.
- Underground Velvet has two recipes: six wool, two sculk and one sculk shrieker yield four; six string, two lapis lazuli and one diamond yield one.
- Mistletoe now uses four flowers and a Bottle o' Enchanting. Everlasting Flower uses four bone meal and four glowstone dust around Spring Corner.

Chest rewards use individual chances of 5%, 7.5%, or 10%, with a total theme reward chance of 10%–20% per chest and at most one theme reward per roll.

## Activation Changes

- Golden Age and Book of the Dead now work while carried. Book of the Dead still requires The Long Travail to be equipped.
- Don Quixote, Voice of the Stars, and Phosphophyllite can now be equipped and take effect in any functional accessory slot, but cannot be placed in cosmetic slots. Only one of each can be equipped at a time; Voice of the Stars and Phosphophyllite still require The Long Travail to be equipped as well.

## Language Support

Added **Traditional Chinese (Taiwan)**, **Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong)**, and **Classical Chinese (Huaxia)**, available through the game's language settings. Thanks to [zgxhzhr](https://github.com/zgxhzhr) for contributing the translations in [PR #1](https://github.com/3101516445z-dotcom/The-Long-Travail/pull/1).

Refined the Classical Chinese descriptions of carrying items, maximum health, the proportion of health lost, and effect ranges to match their actual behavior.

Added 12 vanilla default structures, two per aspect, with the default structure draw count set to 2. Flourishing includes a plains village and a woodland mansion. Compatibility presets expand the candidate pools; existing configurations and journeys are preserved.

## Mod Compatibility

The relevant Gravestone, Caelus, and Apotheosis adapters now check compatibility against the actual interfaces. If a check fails, the mod logs a warning and disables the affected adapter, preventing these interface mismatches from directly aborting game startup.

- **Gravestone**: Disabling the integration pauses The Long Travail's grave item reconciliation while preserving grave contents and existing records. Compatibility is checked again after restoring a compatible version and restarting the game.
- **Caelus**: An incompatible interface disables the flight adapter. Icarus then uses its flight checks without that adapter.
- **Apotheosis anvils**: When Apotheosis is installed, it handles anvil level limits to avoid overlapping changes. The Long Travail's cost cap, free item pickup, and name styling remain independently active.

Added biome presets for Terralith (65 candidates across all six themes), Incendium (8 for Underworld), and Alex's Caves (6 biomes across Flourishing, Abyss, Deep Valley, and Underworld). Terralith and Incendium support both mod and data pack installations. On first detection, available biomes are added with weight 1 and names resolved through language resources. Existing settings for the same IDs are preserved; deleted entries are not automatically restored after initialization.

Added 82 candidate structures from 14 mods as theme-specific presets. Structure and biome initialization are tracked independently, preserving manual settings. Structure draw counts remain controlled by the existing configuration. Acropolis belongs to Abyss; Maid Spell uses Hidden Cherry Tree, Elven Realm, and Fallen Sanctum, while Alex's Caves adds only biome presets.

Expanded the vanilla defaults for Underworld fishing and Boundless phantom rewards to 12 items, adding nautilus shells, netherite scraps and echo shards while adjusting basic-material weights. Added 49 reward candidates from 8 mods with preset weights and quantities. Missing items are appended on first detection, with separate defaults, compatibility presets and initialization for each pool; existing manual settings are preserved.

Updated the 9-entry vanilla fishing entity default pool, replacing pufferfish with villagers and adjusting weights. Added 15 entity candidates from Youkai's Feasts, Youkai's Homecoming, Ars Nouveau, Ars Elemental and Touhou Little Maid, preserving manual weights with separate initialization. Fishing trigger chances are unchanged.

Added 36 beneficial-effect candidates from 12 sources to each of the Far Reach and Deep Valley pools, with weight 1 and per-effect level caps. Each pool initializes independently and adds only registered beneficial effects, preserving manual settings and existing vanilla default weights.

Added Golden Age compatibility blacklists: when their mods are installed, Celestial Fruit and Abyssal Stew are excluded from food rewards, while L2 Complements' six immunity enchantments and Celestial Enchantments' Death Blade and Celestial Shelter are excluded from enchantment rewards. The lists initialize separately, preserve existing settings and do not restore entries removed afterward.

Youkai's Homecoming and Youkai's Feasts have separate entity and beneficial-effect presets with their own namespaces and initialization records. Existing Feasts initialization does not prevent presets from being added when Homecoming is installed later.

## Perfect Fluid

- **Sophisticated Backpacks**: Copying is refused for backpacks containing captured mobs and supported containers that contain such backpacks at any nesting depth. Originals and crafting ingredients are preserved. This restriction applies automatically without configuration blacklist entries; empty backpacks and backpacks with an unused Mob Catcher upgrade are unaffected by this restriction.
- **Beyond Dimensions**: Added copying support for matter compression balls, preserving supported resource quantities and creating independent copies of supported nested inventories. Blacklist filtering and special inventory restrictions still apply. Crafting must use the vanilla player crafting grid or crafting table; the dimensional network itself is not duplicated.
- **Descriptions**: Updated the copying scope to “almost any item” and clarified that ingredients are retained when copying is refused due to a special inventory, across all five languages.

## Configuration and Loot

### Reading and Managing Configuration

Reorganized configuration files: each theme starts with its biome and structure pools, while preset processing records appear at the end, grouped by type and labeled with the corresponding mods. Layout changes preserve existing keys, values and list order.

Shortened repeated comments, clarified units and the distinction between ratios and flat attribute bonuses, and explained that preset records control further automatic additions rather than enabling or disabling features. Clarified the per-item carrying location switches for Eki and Tower of Babel.

Reorganized both configuration guides into syntax, presets and other mechanics. Added basic syntax, list formats, an introduction to presets and instructions for retrying or skipping additions. Section codes in configuration comments lead to the relevant detailed rules.

### Recovery and Validation

Improved handling of invalid configuration files. If a file cannot be parsed at startup, its original contents and an error report are saved under `config/the_long_travail/invalid/` before restoring that file's defaults. Settings in other configuration files are preserved.

- **Validation**: Supports UTF-8 files with a BOM and valid numeric strings. Invalid ordinary settings use their defaults at startup or retain effective values during the current session. Runtime edits that cannot be parsed remain on disk for repair.
- **Loot isolation**: An invalid table no longer blocks other valid tables. It retains its effective rules from the current session, or disables its rewards if none exist. The fishing treasure subtable chance is handled independently; explicit disabling and deletion still take effect.
- **Reloading and notices**: Supports both direct writes and file replacement by editors, with a single summary notice for single-player users or server administrators. Duplicate warnings are suppressed, error records have a fixed capacity, and warning bursts use limited detail output with summaries of omitted counts and affected files.

## Acquisition Configuration Update

For existing configurations, remove `loot.tables."minecraft:gameplay/fishing"`, update the chest entries above, and replace `loot.perfect_fluid.fishingChance` with `loot.fishing_treasure.itemChance = 0.1`. Save and run `/reload` or restart.

## High Frequency Tremor

- Added a Deep Valley netherite-tier pickaxe with doubled mining speed and no further durability loss.
- While wearing The Long Travail, sneak-use mines normally with doubled Fortune level and Luck for loot calculation with Malice; Witness can break normally unbreakable blocks outside its configurable blacklist (only barriers by default), with normal drops.
- Supports FTB Ultimine selections and respects protection events. Normal loot rules apply without extra copies of items, inventories or experience.
- Shaped crafting uses two Deep Valley Essences, Azrael, a Netherite Pickaxe and an Immortal Bud, inheriting the material pickaxe's tag and restoring full durability.

## Default Malice values

- Reduced the default high-altitude movement speed penalty from Boundless Malice and the healing received penalty from Flourishing Malice from 80% to 50%. Existing configuration values are preserved.

## Text and configuration descriptions

- Standardized the Simplified Chinese maid-accessory slot notice without a final full stop.
- Refined the Chinese descriptions of Liquefied Abundance hold and fallback durations and the player-wide cooldown, and clarified that the High Frequency Tremor Witness blacklist also applies to FTB Ultimine.

## Direct Strike Damage

- Added Direct Strike Damage, which bypasses armor and toughness, enchantment-based damage reduction, Resistance, damage immunity frames, and shields. Absorption, Creative-mode immunity, and vanilla totem protection remain effective. Existing items and skills keep their current damage types.

## Texture Updates

- Updated the status effect icons for Stiff and Visual Deprivation.
