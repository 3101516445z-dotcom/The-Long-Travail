# The Long Travail 1.0.4

Release date: 2026-11-08

## Unreleased additions

### Three combat effects

- Added the neutral effect Mara Transformation: grants a 50% damage bonus and converts damage to Direct Strike. Hits apply Wither III for 5 seconds without replacing stronger Wither. Independently loses 10% of maximum health each second, leaving at least 1 health.
- Added the neutral effect Among All Things: prevents outgoing and incoming damage, direct health loss, and mob hostility. Blocks all standard kill damage by default; this protection can be disabled in configuration.
- Added the harmful effect Revival: independently reduces outgoing damage by 99% and self-propelled movement speed by 50%.
- All three effects have fixed strength, apply to players, maids, and other living entities, and use dedicated icons.

### Purification effects

- Added Physical Purification and Magic Purification, reducing damage from their respective sets of vanilla damage types.
- Each level grants 10% damage reduction. The default cap within TLT is level V; higher levels from external sources are capped at 99% reduction. This reduction multiplies with other percentage reductions.
- Both effects have dedicated icons and currently have no built-in acquisition method.

### Damage calculation

- Consolidated applicable percentage reductions into one multiplicative step, after pending outgoing bonuses and before Insight, Overlord, and absorption. Each effect retains its eligibility rules and individual cap.

## Ancient Strange Tales

- Changed the extra materials required when transferring matching enchantments:
  - A difference of 11–100 levels requires 1 Echo Shard and 1 Music Disc of any kind.
  - A difference above 100 levels requires 1 Echo Shard, 1 Music Disc of any kind, 1 Nether Star and 1 Dragon Head.
- Differences of at most 10 levels still require no extra materials. Other transfer rules remain unchanged.

## Don Quixote

- With Malice active, additional Direct Strike damage now equals 4% of the target's current health before the original hit, instead of 4% of its maximum health. The trigger conditions remain unchanged.
