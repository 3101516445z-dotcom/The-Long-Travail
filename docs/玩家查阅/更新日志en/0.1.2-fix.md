# The Long Travail 0.1.2-fix

Release date: 2026-10-23

This update expands Perfect Fluid storage integration, adds villager trades and a Little Prince drop guarantee, revises diary equipment and tooltips, and fixes flight, copying, and item-state issues.

## The diary and interface

After all six Malices become Witnesses, the diary can be equipped and removed freely in the Travel Diary slot without Homecoming. Any return to Malice restores the removal restriction; creative players can still remove it directly.

Added flavor lines to **24 items**, including Free and Easy Wandering, Voice of the Stars, Don Quixote, and Tower of Babel. They use a wheat-gold gradient with a soft sweep every **8 seconds**. Lines appear after the slot label, or directly after the item name when there is no label, and before functional descriptions.

- **Equipment status:** Added two status lines immediately below the hold-progress bar, without an extra blank line. They use uniform muted cinnabar red before completing all six Witnesses and gray-blue afterward.
- **Diary prose:** The opening now reads “Gaze afar, move forward, record…”, the lines about writing something down and “Ah…” are removed, and the Chinese farewell changes from 「再见了，目之所及的记忆。」 to 「再会，目之所及的记忆。」.

## Trades and acquisition

Added two master-level villager trades with fixed prices and 1 purchase per restock. Existing master villagers receive missing offers without resetting their uses.

| Villager | Price | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Master shepherd | **1 shield and 1 netherite sword** | 1 Don Quixote |
| Master mason | **64 lapis lazuli and 1 Deep Valley Stone** | 1 Phosphophyllite |

- **Mistletoe:** The master farmer price changes from two stacks of bone meal to **64 bone meal and 1 golden apple**. Existing offers update without resetting their uses.
- **Perfect Fluid acquisition:** Removed its crafting recipe. The default source is open-water fishing treasure, yielding 1 item with a **0.25%** base chance per successful catch at zero luck, or **5%** within the treasure pool.

The Little Prince now has a hidden, one-time guarantee per player: **300 rose bush breaks** or **600 poppy breaks** award **1 Little Prince**.

- Replanted flowers count; creative and spectator players are excluded.
- Both thresholds share one guarantee. Earlier random drops do not consume it, and normal random drops continue afterward.
- Progress survives reconnects and respawns; earlier breaks are not counted retroactively.

## Perfect Fluid copying

Supports RS disks and supported addons, standard and portable AE2 item/fluid storage cells, and mixed nesting with Sophisticated Backpacks. Copied inventories are independent and retain supported quantities, charge, names, partitions, and upgrades. Collect copies through the vanilla inventory grid or crafting table; AE2 crafting terminals and automated crafting are not supported.

- **Copy restrictions:** The Long Travail and Perfect Fluid cannot be copied, and configured blacklists apply to nested contents in supported containers. Restricted items are removed only from copies. Bound AE2 spatial cells and creative cells configured for Perfect Fluid or protected inventory items are rejected without consuming ingredients.
- **Perfect Fluid descriptions:** Updated tooltips, catalog entries, and configuration descriptions. Its flavor line reads “Only that which embodies utmost perfection or the deepest soul cannot be copied.” An empty configuration blacklist displays “None”.

This update also fixes shared inventories, truncated quantities, and stale crafting previews:

- Supported disk copies use independent inventories and retain full quantities in large inventories.
- Stale previews cannot be collected after ingredients or conditions become invalid.
- Failed copies attempt to restore ingredients, inventories, and external storage; one restoration failure does not stop the others.

## Flight and movement

Fixed lost flight permission while carrying The Little Prince, unwanted reactivation after stopping in midair, and authorization issues after removal, reconnecting, dimension changes, or respawning. Reduced temporary synchronization-related failures.

- **The Little Prince step climbing:** Fixed inconsistent climbing of one-block steps. The default effective step height is **1.2 blocks**, avoiding repeated refreshes from negligible numerical changes.
- **Boundless Malice:** Fixed residual slow ability flight after leaving high altitude when other flight-speed bonuses change, and brief errors when crossing the height threshold within one tick. Restored verifiable legacy slowdown records.

## Integrations and attributes

- **Apotheosis integration:** Rarity-bearing Long Travail and aspect item names and halos use the rarity color while preserving affixes and custom names.
- **Quark integration:** Unenchanted copies of The Long Travail can accept runes using the existing templates and ingredients. Valid colored runes enable their glint; blank runes hide it. The extension applies only to The Long Travail and is disabled when Quark’s rune module is disabled.
- **Phosphophyllite:** Neither Malice nor Witness boosts Ars Nouveau’s potion-duration attribute, for both players and maids.
- **Overlord:** Raised the attribute cap to approximately **3.4028235 × 10³⁸**, matching Insight. Values above **100%** participate in damage immunity calculations.

## Other fixes and runtime improvements

Reduced repeated queries and temporary objects in Golden Age, carrying checks, Sword and Lantern, and Perfect Fluid copying. Large copies can still cause brief stalls. Added runtime diagnostics, disabled by default, for administrators investigating copy costs and the Everlasting Flower queue.

- **Book of the Dead:** Fixed a potential game or server crash when checking consumption state and related journey records.
- **Golden Age:** Fixed abnormal immersion timers and missing effects in third-party food data. Failed candidate queries retain the gold blocks and may retry on the next tick.
- **The Fifty-three Stations of the Tokaido:** Prevented queued skill requests from before a disconnect, reconnect, or respawn from affecting the replacement player.
- **Tower of Babel:** Fixed missing-data issues during target-memory cleanup; corrupt target records no longer prevent valid records from being processed.
- **Everlasting Flower:** Unequipping cancels pending bonus damage, and re-equipping does not restore the old queue.
- **Myriad Souls Banner and Demon King of the Sixth Heaven:** Fixed repeated updates when attribute values are unchanged.
- **Affection:** Improved binding-state maintenance. Bindings survive entity unloads and resume after reloads, retaining death records needed to clean up offline copies.

## Upgrading

Update clients and servers together to **0.1.2-fix**; existing worlds and journeys require no manual migration.

- **Admin commands:** The admin command root changes from `/the_long_travail` to `/tlt`. The old root is no longer registered; update command blocks and scripts.

Perfect Fluid’s configuration blacklist receives these defaults once for installed integrations:

- **Touhou Little Maid:** initial Soul Spells and those containing a maid; empty Soul Spells are excluded.
- **Touhou Little Maid: Personality:** Monster Battle Device.

The integrations track initialization separately, shared by worlds using the same configuration; existing configurations also receive the defaults once. These blacklist entries remain editable. Removed entries are not added again after initialization, even after reinstalling an integration. To initialize again, set the corresponding flag to `false` and restart.
