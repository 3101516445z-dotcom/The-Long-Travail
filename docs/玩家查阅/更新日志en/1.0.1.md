# The Long Travail 1.0.1

Release date: 2026-11-04

This update fixes item descriptions and catalog displays, improves how several items handle faulty data, and updates RSI support. Item stats, trigger chances and cooldowns are unchanged.

## Item Fixes and Performance

- Golden Age can now keep converting eligible foods even if another food causes an error.
- Immortal Bud cancels crafting and keeps the ingredients when the target item's data cannot be processed correctly.
- Fixed possible freezes or crashes when Midnight Lafite or Spore Blossom processes malformed effects. An effect that cannot be handled safely will not be added.
- Improved performance when maids use Icarus by skipping repeated checks when no sunlight bonus applies. Its effects are unchanged.

## Item Descriptions and Catalog

- Fixed item descriptions sometimes being missing or showing an internal name instead.
- Item descriptions, catalog details and glossary explanations now display English text when a translation is missing.
- Restored the progress bar beneath the hold-to-open catalog hint and fixed cases where it did not display correctly.
- Fixed text display issues in some glossary cards so that adding new terms no longer affects existing cards.
- Moved glossary cards closer to the cursor, including near the edges of the screen.
- Removed outdated tooltip text for Homecoming, Renewal, Wayguide and Coloring, keeping their current descriptions and the diary's dialogue.

## Wording and Languages

- Added an explanation for “Damage from Others”: damage with an attributable source, caused by an entity other than oneself. Star Voice and Azrael now use this term in their relevant descriptions; hover over it to read the explanation.
- Clarified that Star Voice makes a time-stop roll when the wearer deals damage. Its effects are unchanged.
- Improved Ancient Strange Tales' tooltip layout, listed the enchantment-filter ingredients, and highlighted “filter enchantments” in gold for easier reading.
- Updated Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan and Hong Kong), Classical Chinese and English.

## Mod Compatibility

- Updated Perfect Fluid's RSI support to **RSI 1.5.3**. Please update RSI to this version when using the integration.
- Improved copying of unified and resonance disks, supporting larger item and fluid quantities as well as Mana, Source and Soul data. Copies keep the capacity and owner while storing their contents independently of the original.
- Disk copying is refused while a backup is in progress or storage is in an invalid state, preventing incomplete copies.
- Updated integrations involving attributes, teams, gravestones, maid spell health, vein mining and external storage while keeping their existing gameplay rules.
