# The Long Travail 1.0.0

Release date: 2026-11-03

Seven new items bring enchantment transfer, durability protection, random potions and name styling. This update also adds diary poems and structure objectives, and revises equipment balance and acquisition.

## New Content

### Seven New Items

- Ancient Strange Tales: Extract, store and transfer enchantments. Matching enchantments merge at the higher level plus one, up to 255. Honey bottles and fermented spider eyes select non-curse and curse enchantments.

- Immortal Bud: Fully repair an item and grant Evergreen, protecting its durability. Dropped items resist fire, lava, explosions and cactus damage, and do not naturally despawn.

- Liquefied Abundance: Tap to select normal, splash or lingering potions; hold to create a random potion. Supports modded effects and can be crafted or brewed.

- Coloring: Apply or restore nine item-name styles, including whole stacks, without changing item attributes.

- High Frequency Tremor: Mines twice as fast as a netherite pickaxe without durability loss. With The Long Travail equipped, sneak-use boosts Fortune and Luck for drops under Malice, or breaks normally unbreakable blocks outside its blacklist under Witness. Supports FTB Ultimine.

- Sun Chaser: Grants fire and lava immunity while carried, with healing in lava. Malice strengthens projectiles and reflects incoming ones; Witness suppresses healing and hurt cooldowns, with extra direct-strike damage against burning targets. Available from master fletchers or desert archaeology.

- Kagotsurube: A belt that applies or extends Wither on damage. Malice increases Insight and Overlord; Witness converts damage attributed to the wearer into direct strikes.

Sun Chaser and Kagotsurube also work in native maid bauble slots. Their stage abilities follow the Long Travail equipped by the online owner. See the in-game Shiyi catalog for recipes and full effects.

### Diary and Languages

- Travel diary: Added Preface and Inscription poems, with a preface bookmark and independent page scrolling.

- Catalog and tooltips: Standardized base effects, Malice, Witness and active-skill sections. Added hover definitions for direct-strike damage, execution, Insight and other terms.

- Languages: Added Traditional Chinese for Taiwan and Hong Kong, plus Classical Chinese (Huaxia). Thanks to [zgxhzhr](https://github.com/zgxhzhr) for the translations.

- Visuals: Updated the Golden Age texture and the Stiffness and Visual Deprivation effect icons.

## Gameplay Changes

Values below are mod defaults. Changed defaults do not automatically overwrite existing configuration values.

### Activation and Damage Rules

- Golden Age and Book of the Dead: Now work while carried instead of requiring an accessory slot. Book of the Dead still requires The Long Travail to be equipped.

- Myriad Souls Banner: Equipping it and collecting souls no longer require The Long Travail. Theme attribute bonuses still require the matching stage.

- Lorentz Fantasy: Gravity-free arrows and enhanced piercing no longer require The Long Travail; stage abilities still do.

- Don Quixote, Voice of the Stars and Phosphophyllite: Can use any functional accessory slot, with one of each equipped at a time. Voice of the Stars and Phosphophyllite still require The Long Travail.

- Direct-strike damage: Now used by Don Quixote's Malice follow-up, Spring Corner's Rose Bush retaliation and Lorentz Fantasy's Witness follow-up. Bypasses armor, damage reduction, hurt cooldowns, shields and absorption without consuming existing absorption hearts. Creative immunity and vanilla totems still work.

## Acquisition and Exploration

### Recipes and Trades

- Renewal: Replaces Dragon's Breath and Nether Stars with glowstone dust, an echo shard, amethyst shards, a feather, lapis lazuli and black dye.

- Wayguide: Removes the Nether Star; now uses amethyst shards, diamonds, lapis lazuli and an Eye of Ender.

- Golden Age: Netherite ingots become amethyst blocks; lapis lazuli becomes lapis blocks.

- Icarus: Nether Star becomes a daylight detector.

- Mistletoe: The center Flourishing Stone becomes a Bottle o' Enchanting.

- Everlasting Flower: Four Mistletoe become four bone meal and four glowstone dust; Spring Corner is still required.

- Underground Velvet: Replaces string with wool and increases output from 1 to 16. Adds an alternative recipe yielding one from six string, two lapis lazuli and one diamond.

- Lorentz Fantasy: Trade changes from 64 emeralds + 64 Dragon's Breath to 16 Dragon's Breath + one Dragon Head.

- Phosphophyllite: Replaces the Deep Valley Stone with green dye; still costs 64 lapis lazuli.

Relevant trades on existing master villagers update their ingredients while retaining their use counts.

### Fishing, Archaeology and Chests

- Fishing: Eki and Tokaido move into the treasure subtable at 10% each. Perfect Fluid rises from 5% to 10%, and Abyss Essence from 10% to 20%. These are shares within treasure, not chances per fishing catch.

- Desert archaeology: Don Quixote rises from 5% to 15%, and Sun Chaser is added at 15%. Far Reach Essence in desert wells rises from 15% to 20%.

- Ocean and trail ruins: Removed Don Quixote. Added Perfect Fluid to ocean ruins at 15%. Added Phosphophyllite to common trail ruins at 10%, and increased its rare trail ruin chance from 5% to 20%.

- Chest locations: Book of the Dead moves from stronghold libraries to Nether Fortresses; A Thousand Years Later moves from Nether Fortresses to Woodland Mansions. Removed Free and Easy Wandering from stronghold chests, Azrael from Ancient Cities and Icarus from End Cities. Added Perfect Fluid to shipwreck supply chests.

- Chest chances: Rebalanced theme rewards to individual defaults of 5%, 7.5% or 10%, with a total theme reward chance of 10%–20% per chest.

### Structure Objectives

- Added two vanilla default structures per theme. The default number of structure objectives rises from 0 to 2.

- Supported mods add biome and structure candidates, including Terralith, Incendium and Alex's Caves. Existing configurations and generated journeys are preserved.

## Compatibility and Usability

- Perfect Fluid: Added Beyond Dimensions matter compression ball copying. Refuses to copy Sophisticated Backpacks containing captured mobs, or supported containers holding them, without consuming materials. Empty backpacks are unaffected.

- Rewards and effects: Expanded defaults and mod presets for Underworld fishing, Boundless phantoms, fished entities and Far Reach / Deep Valley beneficial effects. Added Golden Age food and enchantment exclusions for supported mods.

- Optional integrations: Improved Gravestone, Caelus and Apotheosis anvil compatibility. Incompatible interfaces disable the affected integration with a notice instead of aborting startup.

- Combat compatibility: Improved A Thousand Years Later's undead checks with mods that replace vanilla enchantment behavior.

- Configuration recovery: Backs up damaged files before restoring their defaults. An invalid loot table no longer blocks other valid tables, and configuration errors are summarized.

- Configuration readability: Biome and structure pools come first, with preset records grouped together. Improved units, list syntax and bilingual guides.

- Display fixes: Fixed dream cooldown and time-stop visuals lingering after leaving a world on some Forge versions. Invalid resource-pack textures for Icarus fall back to its bundled texture.

## Before Updating

- Worlds and configuration: Back up before updating. Journey data generated or reset after this update cannot be downgraded. Existing configurations do not automatically adopt all new defaults.

- Ability settings: Sword and Lantern gains configurable crowd size and detection radius. Wayguide's search radius accepts 16–30000000 blocks, defaulting to 5000. Tokaido's Call Rain no longer has a disable switch.

- Peak settings: Icarus and Midnight Lafite now calculate special-environment peaks as the ordinary peak plus an extra increment. Old values migrate automatically; both parameters and the Witness bonus have a minimum of 0.01.

- Archaeology protection: Enabled by default, this skips Forge global loot modifiers applied after archaeology rolls. Packs that depend on those modifiers can disable it.

- Old fishing configuration: Remove `loot.tables."minecraft:gameplay/fishing"` and replace `loot.perfect_fluid.fishingChance` with `loot.fishing_treasure.itemChance = 0.1`. The new value is each item's share of treasure for Eki, Tokaido and Perfect Fluid. Run `/reload` or restart after editing; update old chest tables separately to adopt the new distribution.
